💡 CLAUDE TIP OF THE DAY — Verifiable Done-Criteria

End every Claude prompt with a checklist it must satisfy before stopping.

Instead of:
"Write product descriptions for these 10 SKUs"

Write:
"Write product descriptions for these 10 SKUs.
You are done when:
✅ All 10 descriptions are ≤150 words
✅ Each includes the primary keyword
✅ Output is a JSON array I can paste into Shopify"

This turns "I think it's done" into "it provably is." No back-and-forth, no missed SKUs.
Works for any repeatable workflow — product descriptions, email sequences, ad copy batches.
